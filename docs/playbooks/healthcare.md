# Healthcare Playbook

**Problem solved:** Less documentation and billing burden; clearer patient communication.

## Applicable prompts

| Prompt | Subsection | Risk | Registry ID |
|---|---|---|---|
| SOAP Note Draft | clinical_documentation | high | `healthcare.clinical_documentation.soap_note_draft` |
| Plain-Language Discharge Instructions | patient_communication | high | `healthcare.patient_communication.discharge_instructions_plain` |
| Claim Denial Appeal Letter | revenue_cycle | medium | `healthcare.revenue_cycle.denial_appeal_letter` |

## Risk and compliance
PHI/HIPAA/GDPR: de-identify first; clinician sign-off; no unstated diagnoses.

## Before / after
Visit transcript -> SOAP draft with 'unclear' items flagged for the clinician.

## What to measure
Documentation minutes per visit, clinician edit rate, denial overturn rate, readability grade.

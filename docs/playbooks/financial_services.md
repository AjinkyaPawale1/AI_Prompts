# Financial Services Playbook

**Problem solved:** Faster, consistent credit, AML and research workflows with auditable outputs.

## Applicable prompts

| Prompt | Subsection | Risk | Registry ID |
|---|---|---|---|
| Earnings Call Digest | advisory | medium | `financial_services.advisory.earnings_call_digest` |
| AML Alert Triage | compliance | high | `financial_services.compliance.aml_alert_triage` |
| Credit Memo Summary | credit | high | `financial_services.credit.credit_memo_summary` |

## Risk and compliance
Model risk (SR 11-7 style governance), AML audit trails, explainability, no autonomous decisions.

## Before / after
Credit memo: analyst hand-writes 2h -> draft in minutes with explicit missing-data list; analyst edits.

## What to measure
Analyst time per memo, rework rate, alert false-positive rate, schema-pass rate.
